# A Unified View of Attention and Residual Sinks: Outlier-Driven Rescaling is Essential for Transformer Training

**论文ID**: 2601.22966
**作者**: Zihan Qiu, Zeyu Huang, Kaiyue Wen, Peng Jin, Bo Zheng, Yuxin Zhou, Haofeng Huang, Zekun Wang, Xiao Li, Huaqing Zhang, Yang Xu, Haoran Lian, Siqi Zhang, Rui Men, Jianwei Zhang, Ivan Titov, Dayiheng Liu, Jingren Zhou, Junyang Lin
**机构**: Qwen Team (Alibaba), University of Edinburgh, Stanford University, Tsinghua University

## 基本信息
- **核心方法**: Outlier-Driven Rescaling（异常值驱动的重缩放）
- **解决的问题**: 探究大语言模型中attention sinks和residual sinks的功能作用，统一解释其起源并提出缓解方法

## 效果评估
- 移除normalization会消除对应的outlier，但会降低训练稳定性和性能
- 直接clipping outlier同时保留normalization会导致性能下降
- Outlier更多作为rescale因子而非贡献者存在
- Outlier可以被吸收到可学习参数中或通过显式门控重缩放缓解
- 训练性能提升平均2 points
- W4A4量化下鲁棒性提升1.2 points
