# SkillsBench: Benchmarking How Well Agent Skills Work Across Diverse Tasks

**论文ID**: 2602.12670
**作者**: Xiangyi Li, Wenbo Chen, Yimin Liu, Shenghan Zheng, Xiaokun Chen, Yifeng He, Yubo Li, Bingran You, Haotian Shen, Jiankai Sun, Shuyi Wang, Qunhong Zeng, Di Wang, Xuandong Zhao, Yuanli Wang, Roey Ben Chaim, Zonglin Di, Yipeng Gao, Junwei He, Yizhuo He, Liqiang Jing, Luyang Kong, Xin Lan, Jiachen Li, Songlin Li, Yijiang Li, Yueqian Lin, Xinyi Liu, Xuanqing Liu, Haoran Lyu, Ze Ma, Bowei Wang, Runhui Wang, Tianyu Wang, Wengao Ye, Yue Zhang, Hanwen Xing, Yiqi Xue, Steven Dillmann, Han-chung Lee
**机构**: 多机构 (中国多所大学 + Karlsruhe Institute of Technology)

## 基本信息
- **核心方法**: 提出SkillsBench benchmark，评估Agent Skills在推理时增强LLM Agent的有效性
- **解决的问题**: Agent Skills快速采用但没有标准方法衡量其实际效果

## Benchmark设计
- 86个任务，11个领域
- 配套curated Skills和确定性验证器
- 三种评估条件：无Skills、curated Skills、自生成Skills
- 测试7种agent-model配置，共7,308条轨迹

## 关键发现
- Curated Skills平均提升pass rate 16.2个百分点
- 效果因领域差异大：Software Engineering +4.5pp，Healthcare +51.9pp
- 16/84个任务显示负向效果
- 自生成Skills平均无益处——模型无法可靠地创作它们受益的程序性知识
- 2-3个模块的Focused Skills优于综合文档
- 带Skills的小模型可匹配无Skills的大模型
